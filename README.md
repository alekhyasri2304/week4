## 🚀 Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed using **Tableau**.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping analyze overall business performance across products, countries, customer segments, years, discounts, and profitability.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Preview

![Sales Performance Dashboard](Dashboard/Sales_Performance_Dashboard.png)

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance and identify:

- Overall sales and profit performance
- Top-performing products
- Highest-performing countries
- Sales performance across customer segments
- Yearly sales performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Relationship between discounts and sales
- Product-level profitability

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 🌎 Sales by Country
Compares sales performance across different countries.

### 📦 Sales by Product
Shows which products contribute the most to total sales.

### 📈 Sales Trend
Visualizes sales performance across the months of the year.

### 💰 Profit by Product
Compares profitability across individual products.

### 👥 Sales by Segment
Analyzes sales across different customer segments.

### 📅 Sales by Year
Compares sales performance between different years.

### 🎯 Discount vs Sales
Explores the relationship between discounts and sales.
